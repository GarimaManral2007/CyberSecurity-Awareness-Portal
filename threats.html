Cyber Threat Intelligence & Vectors

Know your adversary. Explore active attack methodologies, analyze real-world breach case studies, and discover precision mitigation tactics.

Critical Severity

Spear Phishing & BEC Wire Fraud

Targeted, personalized email scams imitating executives, legal partners, or vendors requesting urgent bank transfers or payroll updates.

  • Deceptive lookalike sender addresses (e.g., @micros0ft.com)
  • Fake urgency ("Wire funds immediately before market close")
  • Compromised vendor invoice account tampering
Vector: Email / SMTP
🦠
Critical Severity

Ransomware & Double Extortion

Malicious payloads that silently exfiltrate company data before encrypting local drives, databases, and network shares demanding millions in cryptocurrency.

  • Spread via weaponized email attachments & unpatched VPNs
  • Exfiltrates confidential records before executing lockups
  • Attempts to destroy online backup volumes first
Vector: Endpoint / RDP
📲
High Severity

MFA Fatigue & Push Bombing

Attackers who possess a stolen password trigger dozens of 2FA push notifications in rapid succession, hoping the exhausted user taps "Approve" to stop the alerts.

  • Covers night-time hours when users are half-asleep
  • Often paired with WhatsApp or SMS pretending to be IT
  • Bypasses standard multi-factor authentication if accepted
Vector: Identity / Mobile
🔓
High Severity

Credential Stuffing & Password Spraying

Automated botnets use leaked email/password combos from historical public breaches to hammer corporate login portals until identical credentials match.

  • Takes advantage of human password reuse across sites
  • Uses rotating proxy IP addresses to evade rate limits
  • Compromises cloud accounts without phishing needed
Vector: Web Auth / Bots
🕵️
Medium Severity

Pretexting & Vishing (Voice Phishing)

Fraudsters phone employees posing as the internal IT Helpdesk, Microsoft Support, or a frantic executive, demanding temporary remote access or MFA one-time codes.

  • Leverages LinkedIn company research to drop real colleague names
  • Uses AI voice cloning to mimic management tone
  • Urges victims to install remote desktop tools (AnyDesk/TeamViewer)
Vector: Voice / Human Trust
📶
Medium Severity

Evil Twin & Public Wi-Fi Sniffing

Threat actors set up fake wireless access points named "Airport_Guest_WiFi" in transit hubs, intercepting unencrypted traffic, cookies, and login credentials.

  • Presents fake captive portals asking for Microsoft 365 login
  • Conducts SSL stripping and session token theft
  • Easily blocked by mandating full-tunnel enterprise VPN
Vector: 802.11 Wireless
📱
Medium Severity

Quishing (QR Code Traps)

Attackers embed malicious QR codes in PDF invoices or physically glue replacement QR stickers over genuine cafeteria or event sign-in boards.

  • Bypasses traditional email gateway URL filters
  • Shifts victim from protected corporate PC to personal mobile browser
  • Redirects to fake credential-harvesting landing pages
Vector: Optical / QR Code
💻
High Severity

Drive-By Downloads & Malvertising

Legitimate mainstream websites unintentionally display compromised ad banners that probe browser versions and deliver silent malware downloads.

  • Requires no direct file execution click by the user
  • Targets outdated browser extensions and PDF plugins
  • Can infect clean corporate devices through web traffic
Vector: HTTP / Browser Engine

Active Breach Incident Response (Step-by-Step)

If you clicked a malicious link, entered credentials, or notice ransomware files, execute this sequence immediately:

Time Critical
Step 01 🔌

Sever Network

Immediately unplug the ethernet cable or turn on Airplane Mode. Do NOT power down the PC (memory contains critical forensic evidence).

Step 02 📸

Preserve Evidence

Take photos of the screen with your smartphone showing any ransom messages, fake update prompts, or altered files. Do not delete files or browser history.

Step 03 📞

Contact SOC Hotline

Call the Security Operations hotline below from an uncompromised mobile phone. State your name, workstation asset tag, and initial symptoms.

Step 04 🔐

Rotate Credentials

From an independent, known-clean device, change your corporate password and terminate all active session tokens across Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace.

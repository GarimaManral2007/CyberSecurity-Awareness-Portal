🏛️ The Three Pillars of Cyber Defense

Many security problems start with a simple mistake. These three habits can help keep your account and devices safe.

🔑
Pillar 01

Protect your account

Use a different, strong password for each account. Turn on sign-in verification when it is available.

  • Use a unique password for each account
  • Turn on two-step verification
  • Decline sign-in requests you did not start
📧
Pillar 02

Check messages carefully

Scam messages may pretend to come from your manager, a supplier, or IT support. They often try to rush you.

  • Check who sent the message
  • Check links before opening them
  • Confirm unusual payment requests by phone
💻
Pillar 03

Keep devices safe

Keep your devices updated and be careful when using public Wi-Fi or unknown USB drives.

  • Install updates when prompted
  • Do not plug in USB drives you find
  • Avoid sensitive work on public Wi-Fi
